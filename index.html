About us

Built on local knowledge, not sales pressure.

Baraka Homes is a Kenyan-owned property company helping families, diaspora buyers and businesses find land, homes and commercial space they can trust. We started in 2012 with a single office and one rule: never list a property we have not personally visited.

Today we cover Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Nakuru, Laikipia, Mombasa and beyond — and the rule has not changed. Whether you are buying your first plot in Juja or a commercial building in Nakuru CBD, you deal with one agent from first enquiry to final handover.

  • Title deeds and land references verified before listing
  • Honest guidance on price, access roads and infrastructure
  • Dedicated agent for diaspora clients, with video viewings
  • Support right through to transfer and handover
